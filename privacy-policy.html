Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Vahweh Design, a women's boutique based in Kerala, India. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Vahweh Design. You can reach us through our Contact Us page.

Information you give us

We receive the information you choose to send us, for example when you message us on WhatsApp or write to us through the contact form: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and the contents of your message or order.

We do not take payments on this website, so we do not ask for card or bank details here.

Information saved in your browser

To make the website work, your browser saves two small items on your own device (using localStorage): the products in your shopping bag and the products you have saved to your wishlist. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it unless you choose to send us your bag, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and enquiries.
  • To confirm, prepare, deliver and support your orders.
  • To keep records we need for accounts, tax and legal reasons.

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we rely on

When you choose to message us, WhatsApp (operated by Meta) handles that conversation under its own terms and privacy policy. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Bootstrap library from the jsDelivr network, so those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when a page loads. Delivery partners receive the name, address and phone number needed to deliver your order.

Keeping and protecting information

We keep order and enquiry details only for as long as we need them to handle your order, answer your questions and meet our accounting and legal obligations, and we take reasonable steps to keep them safe.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep it by law. Contact us and we will reply as soon as we can, usually within 7 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please use our Contact Us page or message us on WhatsApp.
